# DMZ v2.2 Super Saiyan God ritual

The only way to become Super Saiyan God (`godritual` group). It can't be bought, selected or charged.

Requirements: a receiver Saiyan in SSJ Mastered; 5 giver Saiyans in SSJ or SSJ Mastered (no stack form); everyone with a pure heart (alignment 90+ or 10 or less, mixing allowed) and no God Ritual CD effect. The receiver can't be knocked down; a fusion can give but never receive.

How: each giver stands within 5 blocks and gives Ki to the receiver with Ki Transfer (Ctrl+Alt+right-click). Once all five held their link for 1 second it starts by itself ("Six pure hearts resonate as one..."): the givers gather in a ring, everyone channels 10 seconds (nobody can act), the receiver rises and transforms. If something is wrong (impure heart, cooldown, too far), everyone is told why. Damage above 25% interrupts it (30 second cooldown).

Result: worth the receiver's strongest unlocked form with each stat bonus raised 30% (45% for a Mutant), lasts 5 minutes; then a 30 minute cooldown for everyone.

Config: `superSaiyanGodRitual` in general-server.json (`requiredDonors`, `donorForms`, `recipientForms`, `pureGoodMinAlignment`, `pureEvilMaxAlignment`, `durationMinutes`, `cooldownMinutes`, `powerBonus`, `mutantPowerBonus`).
