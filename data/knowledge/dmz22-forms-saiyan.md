# DMZ v2.2 Saiyan forms (Super Saiyan, SSJ2, SSJ3, SSJ4, Oozaru, Legendary, False SSJ, God ritual)

Saiyan Super Forms skill has 8 levels costing 13k, 21k, 31k, 42k, 52k, 65k, 78k, 104k TP. Files in `races/saiyan/forms/`.

- `oozaru.json`: oozaru (no requirement besides a tail and a full moon or fake moon), goldenoozaru (level 7, after buu_saga:1), supersaiyan4 [GT] (level 8, buu_saga:35).
- `ssgrades.json`: supersaiyan (level 1, classic_saga:46), supersaiyangrade2 (2, saiyan_saga:12), supersaiyangrade3 (3, frieza_saga:5).
- `supersaiyan.json`: supersaiyanmastered (4, android_saga:4), supersaiyan2 (5, android_saga:15), supersaiyan3 (6, buu_saga:1), supersaiyan4 [D] (8, buu_saga:35).
- `legendaryforms.json` (shown as the Mutant group): ikari, ssjhybrid, ssjfullpower. Not bought with TP; from Movies Saga quests 30, 34 and 40.
- `falseform.json`: falsesupersaiyan (rage, see the False Super Saiyan doc).
- `godritual.json`: supersaiyangodritual from the six-Saiyan ritual.

Each form in a group needs 25% mastery on the previous one. The two SSJ4 are the Saiyan top tier.
