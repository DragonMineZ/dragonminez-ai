Discord community. By using them, you agree to these Terms.

---

## 1. What the Bot and the Beta Service Are

The Bot is a Discord bot run by the DragonMineZ team. It provides AI support
answers, moderation, tickets, bug report tracking, leveling and other
community tools.

The Patreon beta access service lets eligible patrons get early-access builds
of the DragonMineZ mod. It links your Discord account to your Patreon account
and adds your Minecraft username to the beta whitelist.

---

## 2. Beta Access

- Beta access is a perk of an active Contributor or Benefactor pledge on
  Patreon. Pledges are handled by Patreon.
- Beta access is removed automatically when your pledge ends. Anything you
  gifted is removed with it.
- Each patron gets one whitelist for themselves. Contributors can also gift
  one more whitelist spot, and Benefactors can gift two.
- Early-access and dev builds are for your own use. Do not share, redistribute
  or reupload them.
- These builds can be unstable. They can crash, break worlds or lose data.
  Back up your worlds.
