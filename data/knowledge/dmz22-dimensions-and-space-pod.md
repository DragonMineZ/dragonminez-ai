# DMZ v2.2 dimensions, Space Pod and how to travel

Dimensions: Earth (`minecraft:overworld`), Namek (`dragonminez:namek`), Sacred Kai Planet (`dragonminez:sacredkaiplanet`), Hyperbolic Time Chamber (`dragonminez:time_chamber`, gravity x10, 1.75x TP), Otherworld (`dragonminez:otherworld`), Demon Realm (`dragonminez:demon_realm`, three stacked Demon Worlds, Daima content).

Getting a Saiyan Space Pod: Saiyan Saga quest 15 (Prepare for Namek), a Shenron wish, or crafting (Namek radar chips and CPU, quartz blocks, repeater, minecart, red wool). Right-click a block to place it, right-click to open and climb in; Space rises, Left Alt descends; hit it to pick it up.

Inside the pod press H to pick a destination (you land with the pod):
- Earth, Namek: always (same position).
- Otherworld: Bulma's Otherworld Drive sidequest, Otherworld enabled (lands 54, 210, 1082). Dying also takes you there.
- Time Chamber: Bulma's Temporal Comm-Link sidequest, or the portal at Kami's Lookout.
- Sacred Kai Planet: Buu Saga quest 23.
- Demon Realm: Daima Saga quest 3 (lands high above the Third Demon World).
Cereal and Beerus Planet are greyed out (reserved for add-ons).

Custom destinations: datapack `data/<namespace>/spacepod/*.json` with a `destinations` list (`id`, `dimension`, optional `x`/`y`/`z`, `unlock_rules`: `{"quest": ...}`, `{"level": N}`, `race`, `visited_dimension`, `and`/`or`/`not`, ALWAYS, NEVER); `"replace": true` overrides earlier files. Vanilla `/reload` applies them.
