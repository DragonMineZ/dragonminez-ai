# DMZ v2.2 Majin racial skills: Absorption, Sweet Tooth, Healing Magic

- Absorption: select the racial skill in the X menu and hold G for 4 seconds aiming at a weaker target within 8 blocks (mobs allowed by default) or a knocked-down player. You heal 30% of max HP and permanently gain 4% of the target's STR, SKP and PWR (mobs use their max health). Up to 3 absorptions, 600 second cooldown. Ejecting one from the X menu locks that slot 300 seconds. You lose all absorptions when you die. While selected, finishing blows knock enemies down instead of killing.
- Sweet Tooth: food heals 25% more.
- Healing Magic: healing ki techniques heal 25% more, you receive 25% more from them, and they cost 10% less Ki and recharge 10% faster.

Config: `racialSkills.majin` in general-server.json (`absorptionAmount`, `absorptionCooldownSeconds`, `absorptionStatCopy`, `absorptionOnMobs`, `slotEjectCooldownSeconds`, `foodHealBonus`, `kiHeal*`).

Keywords: majin racial, absorption, absorb, buu, sweet tooth, healing magic, majin passive, eject absorption
