# DMZ v2.2 beam clash, strike clash and power clash

Beam clash: starts when two beams (Wave and Beam types: Kamehameha, Galick Gun, Final Flash, Special Beam Cannon, custom Wave attacks...) from different fighters meet head-on. Small balls, lasers and disks don't clash; they are destroyed by a beam. Allies (party with PvP off, same team, same world boss fight) never clash. Both fighters are held in place and invulnerable.

Timing meter: a marker sweeps (2 to 3 seconds per sweep) and each sweep has a window. Right-click inside it: near the centre is PERFECT, elsewhere GOOD, outside is MISS. One press per sweep, checked by the server. Presses build momentum; stats decide how hard it pushes, but timing can beat a stronger fighter.

End: the side that pushes far enough breaks through; the loser is EXHAUSTED (stunned 3.5 seconds). It dissolves if someone stops firing or nobody presses for 7 seconds; after 30 seconds whoever is ahead wins. NPC enemies clash too.

Strike clash: two fighters rushing each other with strike attacks use the same meter.

Power clash (world bosses): Gomah's sphere and Metal Cooler's Supernova start a group clash; fire a beam at it to join and press on the marks.
