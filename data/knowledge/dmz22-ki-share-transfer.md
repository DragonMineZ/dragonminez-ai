# DMZ v2.2 Ki Transfer (giving Ki to players, Spirit Bomb help)

Look at a player within 8 blocks and hold Ctrl + Alt + right-click (Use). Letting go ends the link; 2 seconds before a new one. You can't give while receiving, to someone giving their own Ki, or while stunned, knocked down or out of Ki.

Giver: sends 2.5% of max Ki per second, moves at 40% speed, can't attack, block, charge, transform or use techniques, and gets Ki Exhaustion: -5% STR, SKP, DEF, PWR every 3 seconds (max -25%), lasting 5 seconds per 3 seconds given after the link ends.

Receiver: gets the Ki, and after 1 second each giver adds +5% STR, SKP, DEF and PWR (Shared Ki, up to 5 givers = +25%), kept 5 seconds per 3 seconds given. Ki beyond max is lost unless the receiver is charging a ki attack (stored for it, up to one full bar).

The link breaks if the giver takes 10% of max HP in damage, is stunned, the players are over 32 blocks apart or in different dimensions, or someone dies or logs out.

Spirit Bomb: players within 64 blocks are asked to help; hold the same keys (no need to look at the caster). Givers send 10% max Ki per second, the bomb grows up to 2.5x and deals up to x3 damage, and the link reaches 64 blocks.

Five Saiyans giving Ki to a sixth can start the Super Saiyan God ritual. Config: `kiTransfer` in general-server.json.
