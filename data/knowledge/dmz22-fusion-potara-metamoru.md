# DMZ v2.2 fusion: Fusion Dance (Metamoru), Potara Earrings, Medi-Bug Beetles

Two players merge; the leader controls the body and the partner rides along. Bonus STR, SKP and PWR from the partner's stats. Lasts 15 minutes by default (`fusionDurationSeconds`; the Dance scales with Fusion skill level), ends early on death, disconnect or game mode change.

- Fusion Dance: both need the Fusion skill (Goku teaches it), same race and same form, stat gap under 50% (`metamoruFusionThreshold`). Both select Fusion in the Utility menu (X) and hold G close together; whoever fills the charge first leads. x1.25 to x2.0. Upgraded Androids can't. Both get the 30 minute Fusion cooldown (`fusionCooldownSeconds`).
- Potara Earrings (Porunga wish, yellow or green): any races, both wear matching earrings and get near each other; right earring leads. x1.75 to x2.5. The earrings break afterwards.
- Medi-Bug Beetles (Demon Realm traders, Medi-Bug Market sidequest): any races, both eat matching beetles within 30 seconds and 20 blocks; first to eat leads. x1.25 to x2.0.

While fused: leader's race, class and stats plus the bonus; higher skill levels and form masteries of the two; techniques of both; with different races the leader can use the partner's race forms (not Android forms). Blended colors and names. Partner gets half of the TP earned. You can't buy skills, edit techniques or take the Android upgrade while fused.
