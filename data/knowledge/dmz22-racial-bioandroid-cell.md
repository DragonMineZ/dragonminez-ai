# DMZ v2.2 Bio-Android racial skills: Vital Drain, Self-Destruct, Cell Jr.

The Bio-Android racial skill changes with your form:
- Imperfect: Vital Drain.
- Semi-Perfect: Vital Drain or Self-Destruct.
- Perfect: Summon Cell Jr.

Vital Drain: select it in the X menu and press G next to a target within 3 blocks. You appear behind it and both are stunned while you drain 25% of its max HP per second, healing that and gaining five times as much Ki. Draining to 1 HP kills it (grants TP in Imperfect form). Cooldown 180 seconds.

Self-Destruct (Semi-Perfect): hold G 5 seconds and release to explode. Everything within 36 blocks takes up to 500% of your max HP as damage (less with distance) and terrain is destroyed. By default it does not kill you but knocks you down 30 seconds (`explodeKillsUser`). Cooldown 600 seconds.

Cell Jr. (Perfect): hold G to summon one with 20% of your HP and damage, up to 3 at a time (30 server-wide). Each living Cell Jr. lowers your stats 10%; a destroyed one's slot recharges in 240 seconds. Alt+G dismisses all.

Config: `racialSkills.bioandroid` in general-server.json.
