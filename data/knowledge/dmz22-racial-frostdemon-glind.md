# DMZ v2.2 Frost Demon and Glind racial skills

Frost Demon:
- Prodigious Strength: 25% more TP from every source (stacks with Time Chamber etc.).
- Evolutionary Mastery: forms gain 25% more mastery.
- Hardened Body: 12.5% less damage from ki techniques, 5% less from strike techniques.
- Power Reserve: in base form or second/third/final form with Power Release at 25% or less, Ki is stored in a reserve up to 50% of max Ki (full in 30 seconds). Transformed at 100% Power Release, the reserve powers the form up to 10 seconds: no Ki upkeep and 15% stronger form bonuses.
Config: `racialSkills.frostdemon`.

Glind:
- Divine Ki: Ki Sense, scouters and Instant Transmission cannot find you unless the seeker has a God form or Kami's Blessing. Lock On still works.
- Celestial Body: HP, Ki and stamina regen 25% higher; Meditation melee dodge chance 50% higher.
- Divine Punishment: your hits reduce players' healing by 20% for 6 seconds (Healing Reduction skill stacks at 50%, up to 40%).
- Light and Darkness: +10% damage to players of opposite alignment (+15% vs Frost Demons, +5% vs Majins); +7.5% against any non-player.
Config: `racialSkills.glind`.
