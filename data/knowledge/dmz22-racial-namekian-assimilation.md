# DMZ v2.2 Namekian racial skills: Assimilation, Regeneration, Water Affinity

- Assimilation: select the racial skill in the X menu and hold G for 4 seconds aiming at another Namekian (player or Namek NPC) within 3 blocks. Players must accept (30 second request) unless they are knocked down. You heal 35% of max HP and permanently gain 7.5% of your own STR, SKP and PWR. Up to 4 assimilations, 600 second cooldown; you lose the latest one when you die. While selected, your finishing blows knock enemies down instead of killing them.
- Regeneration: from the X menu, recover 25% of max HP over 3 seconds, costing 30% Ki and 20% stamina. Damage interrupts it. Cooldown 45 seconds (half if interrupted).
- Water Affinity: being underwater or drinking any potion boosts HP, Ki and stamina regen by 25% for 5 seconds.

Config: `racialSkills.namekian` in general-server.json (`assimilationAmount`, `assimilationCooldownSeconds`, `assimilationStatBoost`, `assimilationOnNamekNpcs`, `regen*`, `waterRegen*`).

Reset with `/dmzracial reset` or the racial reset wish.
