# DMZ v2.2 flight, dash, Instant Transmission, lock on, Ki Sense

Flight: press F. It needs the Fly and Ki Control skills and at least 5% Power Release to take off (otherwise "You need the Ki Control skill"). Alt+F switches between Combat Fly and Search Fly; the mode, speed cap and mode lock are also in the Utility menu (X) under Movement > Fly. Combat Fly is slower but agile, has impulses (5% Ki each) and half the drain. With `combatFlyAutoSwitchOnDamage` you switch to Combat Fly when hit and stay in it 8 seconds. Client options: `cameraMovementDuringFlight`, `flightTurnSensitivity`.

Techniques also need the Ki Control skill.

Dash: R. Cooldown 4 seconds, double dash 12 seconds (`dashCooldownSeconds`, `doubleDashCooldownSeconds` in combat.json).

Instant Transmission (skill taught by Goku): tap H to teleport to your locked-on target; hold H to open the destination menu (IT level 5+). Range to players grows 200 blocks per level and Ki cost rises with distance. Glinds (Divine Ki) cannot be targeted unless you have a God form or Kami's Blessing. A tap shares the dash cooldown; warping through the IT menu has a 30 second cooldown (`teleportCooldownSeconds`).

Lock on: Z. Ki Sense: F4 cycles Combat mode, Search mode and off (needs the Ki Sense skill).
