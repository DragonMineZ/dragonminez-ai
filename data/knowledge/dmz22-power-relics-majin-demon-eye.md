# DMZ v2.2 power items and buffs: Might Fruit, Majin Mark, Sacred Water, Demon Eye, Kami's Blessing

Settings in general-server.json `gameplay`.
- Fruit of the Tree of Might: x1.2 STR, SKP and PWR (half to defense) for 1 minute (`mightFruitPower`). From the Tree of Might, Turles (50%) and Shenron (16).
- Majin Mark: permanent x1.3 (half to defense, `majinPower`) from Babidi in Babidi's Ship if your alignment is below 39. Holds alignment at 0. Admins: `/dmzeffect give majin -1` / `remove majin`.
- Sacred Water: permanent x1.1 to all stats, once per character, survives death (Korin's quest in Classic Saga quest 19; `sacredWaterMultiplier`).
- Demon Eye (Head Tech Curios slot, Gomah 2% drop): x1.5 all stats but drains 2% max Ki per second (then HP, never lethal), -1 alignment every 10 seconds, grows you up to x1.5 size over 5 minutes. 3 hits from behind within 10 seconds knock it off (`demonEye*`).
- Kami's Blessing (Dende at Kami's Lookout, Good alignment): x1.2 TP for 5 minutes, 30 minute cooldown (`kamiBless*`); also lets you sense Glinds.
- Hero of the World: +25% TP for 10 minutes after winning a raid.
