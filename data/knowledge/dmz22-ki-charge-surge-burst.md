# DMZ v2.2 ki charging, Power Release, Ki Burst, Ki Surge, Ki Protection, Ki Infusion

Charge Ki: hold C to raise Power Release up to your limit; double-tap C to jump to max. Power Release multiplies damage, defense, speed and Battle Power, so fighting uncharged leaves you exposed. A hit while charging stops the charge (and surge progress) and blocks charging for half a second. While charging, arrows and other non-ki projectiles are deflected. Alt+C lowers release; Limit Release in the Utility menu caps it.

Ki Burst: the moment you reach full Ki while charging, enemies within 8 blocks with lower Battle Power are pushed back.

Ki Surge: keep charging at full Ki for 4 seconds (letting go empties the bar). On entry a burst damages and blows back everyone within 10 blocks for up to a quarter of your max Ki; it can never kill. For 12 seconds: ki attacks +15% damage and +25% speed, technique charge +20%, move/fly +25%, attack speed +40%. Ki costs +25% and spending Ki drains the bar. Stuns end it. 15 second cooldown.

Ki Protection (toggle): -2.5% damage per level (max 25%), paying Ki equal to 15% of the incoming damage. Ki Infusion (toggle): +2.5% of max Ki per level as melee damage (max +25%), costing 2.5% to 7.5% of max Ki per hit. Toggle in the Skills menu or Ki Actions in the Utility menu.

Settings in combat.json (`enableKiSurge`, `surge*`, `kiBurst*`, `kiProtection*`, `kiInfusion*`).
