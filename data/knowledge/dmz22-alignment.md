# DMZ v2.2 alignment (good, neutral, evil) and what it affects

Alignment is 0 to 100; every character starts at 100. Bands: Good 61-100, Neutral 41-60, Evil 0-40. Shown in the Stats tab.

What changes it:
- Killing a player with alignment 50+: -5; below 50: +2. Killing villagers, Namekian warriors and traders, Demon Realm villagers, the Time Patroller: -5. Killing Red Ribbon, Frieza soldiers, bandits: +2.
- Losing raids: Saiyan Assault -10, Frieza's Invasion -20, Gomah's Army -30.
- Winning tournaments: Baba +5, Cell Games +10, Otherworld +10.
- Quest rewards and dialogue choices; Demon Eye -1 every 10 seconds; the Majin Mark holds it at 0.
- Admins: `/dmzalignment set|add|remove <amount> [targets]`.

What it affects:
- Good masters (Goku, Gohan, King Kai, Old Kai, Roshi, Krillin) need 61+; evil masters (Cell, Frieza) need 40 or less; others (Piccolo, Vegeta, Trunks, Yamcha, Baba, Buu...) none. Locked masters still talk but keep Skills, Train and new quests locked.
- Korin: Flying Nimbus above 50, otherwise Black Nimbus. Dende: Kami's Blessing at 61+. Grand Elder Guru: Unlock Potential (Potential Unlock 10 to 11) at 50+. Babidi: Majin Mark below 39. Old Kai: Ultimate Challenge at 62+ with Potential Unlock 10.
- Hell ogres attack Evil players on sight; Glind racial bonuses depend on alignment.
